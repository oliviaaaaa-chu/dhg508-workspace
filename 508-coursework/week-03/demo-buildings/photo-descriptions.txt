Descriptions of the six building photos in images/
(Each description is based on viewing the photo itself; match to the
buildings.db record follows at the end of each entry.)
========================================================================

bld-01.jpg (id 1)
----------
A long, three-story brick building, wider than tall, photographed
straight-on from across an open grassy field. Ground floor: a continuous
arcade of round arches running the full length of the facade. Upper two
floors: open colonnaded balconies with regular rows of columns. Every
level is crowded with students standing along the colonnades. Flat
roofline with a row of chimneys/vent stacks; fully Western/colonial
style, no Chinese roof. Foreground is empty grass; overcast sky; sepia
tone, likely 1900s-1910s.

Match: id 1 - Martin Hall (马丁堂), 1906-1907. Lingnan's first permanent
building and China's first reinforced-concrete mixed-structure building;
designed by Stoughton & Stoughton. (LUAR_PIC image 1001)

bld-02.jpg (id 2)
----------
An interior shot, not an exterior: a large reading room. Long wooden
tables covered with books, papers and newspapers. Rows of students
(young men in white shirts) seated, reading and writing. Tall bookshelves
packed with bound volumes line both side walls; square brick piers rise
between them. Pendant lamps hang from the ceiling on long cords; tall
windows in the background. Busy 1920s-era library reading room, sepia.

Match: id 2 - Grand Hall (格蘭堂), 1915-1916. Nicknamed the "Big Clock
Tower" (大钟楼); its east second floor long housed the library.
(LUAR_PIC image 1004)
Note: hard case for a text-matching ("blind") agent - no exterior is
visible; only the DB description reveals it is inside Grand Hall.

bld-03.jpg (id 3)
----------
A large multi-story building in Chinese revival style. Layered
hip-and-gable tiled roofs: a big central ridge plus stepped, receding
roof tiers on each side, with small dormer-like structures along the
ridgeline. A pale vertical panel at the center of the facade. Regular
rows of windows across four floors; the two lower floors have arcaded
loggias with arched openings; small balconies with railings at the
sides. Brick, symmetrical, wings stepping down from the tall central
section. Foreground: lawn/road with shrubs and a palm-like plant, a
person walking at bottom right. Sepia.

Match: id 3 - Java Hall (爪哇堂), 1920-1922. First student dormitory,
funded by overseas Chinese in Java; four stories, completed 1922.
(LUAR_PIC image 1011)

bld-04.jpg (id 4)
----------
An octagonal pavilion rather than a large building: pyramidal tiled roof
(八角攒尖顶), ridges sweeping up from eight corners to a finial, with
pronounced upturned eaves. Open colonnade of wooden columns beneath; a
large bronze bell hangs inside. Raised stone terrace with carved
balustrade, steps at the front. Stands in the middle of a broad lawn
with light cross-paths; two Western-style buildings flank it (brick
building left, lighter three-story building right). Sepia tone.

Match: id 4 - Xing Pavilion (惺亭), 1928. Funded by the 1928 graduating
class "惺社" to commemorate three martyrs (史坚如, 区励周, 许耀章);
designed by Henry Murphy; the bell was struck to signal class times.
(LUAR_PIC image 1010)
Note: near-duplicate of the exercise's query/query.jpg.

bld-05.jpg (id 5)
----------
A tall steel lattice/truss water tower, photographed from below.
Cylindrical water tank perched on top - a plain metal drum wider than
the slender tower beneath. Four legs braced by repeated X-shaped
cross-bracing with horizontal ties; a ladder runs up the frame; legs
widen at the base onto a low masonry plinth behind a fence. Dark trees
frame both sides; the bottom edge shows water with the tower's
reflection. Darker, more contrasty tone than the other photos.

Match: id 5 - Water Tower (自来水塔), year unknown. Campus water-supply
tower inside the school gate; the DB's thinnest record - no date, no
known designer, one line of history. (LUAR_PIC image 1012)

bld-06.jpg (id 6)
----------
A row of simple, single-story wooden bungalows stretching across a
raised slope, several units joined end-to-end. Low, shallow-pitched
roofs with small chimneys/vents; wide verandas and covered walkways in
front. Light timber-plank walls - clearly temporary, not masonry.
Rural, undeveloped setting: rough scrub and cultivated field plots in
the foreground, trees at both ends, tiny figures at far right. Sepia,
panoramic feel.

Match: id 6 - Log Cabins (木板屋), 1904. Earliest temporary schoolhouses
of Lingnan College after moving to the Kanglecun site; at the time the
whole school was just two large wooden buildings, predating every other
structure in the database. Source: 1913 alumni account (LUAR_PIC
image 1000)
