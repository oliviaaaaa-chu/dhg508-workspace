# Source: The China Who's Who (1922), pp. 147–148

## What this is

Scan pages of Johnston's entry from *The China Who's Who: A Biographical
Dictionary of ... * compiled by Carroll Lunt, Shanghai, 1922 — a
contemporary (1922) English-language biographical dictionary published in
China. Printed pages [147]–[148], scan leaves n157–n158.

## Key text (as visible on the scan, p. 147)

> **Johnston, Reginald Fleming, C.B.E.** (Peking), Tutor to H.M. the Emperor
> Hsuan Tung: b. in Scotland: educ. Edinburgh University; Magdalen College,
> Oxford: ... Administered the Government of Weihaiwei 1917-1918: Created
> C.B.E. 1918: **In 1919 accepted the invitation of the Chinese Government
> to become Tutor to the ex-Emperor of China** ...

Entry tail (top of p. 148): "add. 46 Tsai Chang Hutung: n. British."

## Why it matters for the question

Contemporary published record (1922), only ~3 years after the event,
stating that in **1919** Johnston "accepted the invitation of the Chinese
Government" to become tutor to the ex-Emperor. Corroborates Puyi's
autobiography (introduction ceremony 4 March 1919 at Yuqing Palace) and
Johnston's own *Twilight in the Forbidden City* (1934). Note it frames the
hiring as an invitation of the *Chinese Government* (negotiated via
President Xu Shichang / Li Jingmai), not the British Colonial Office.
